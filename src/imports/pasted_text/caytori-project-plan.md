1. Project Name
Caytori — IT Services Ticketing & Support Management Platform
Tagline:
Centralize IT Support. Resolve Issues. Keep Business Moving.

2. Project Description
Caytori is a multi-company web-based IT support platform that centralizes employee IT concerns into one structured ticketing system. Companies can configure their organization, onboard employees and IT personnel, receive and assign IT service requests, communicate with employees, track issue resolution, and monitor IT support performance through dashboards and reports.
Instead of employees reporting technical problems through scattered channels such as Messenger, email, verbal requests, or personal messages, Caytori provides a centralized process where:
Employees report → IT Support receives → IT Staff handles → Employee confirms → Caytori records and analyzes.
The platform is designed to work for companies of different sizes, whether they have one IT employee, a small IT team, or a dedicated IT department.

3. Problem Statement
Companies commonly encounter IT concerns such as:
Computers not working
Internet/network problems
Software errors
Account access issues
Email problems
Printer problems
Hardware failures
Security concerns
When these concerns are reported informally, companies can lose track of:
Who reported the issue
When it was reported
Who is handling it
How serious it is
Whether it has been resolved
How long resolution took
Which departments experience the most problems
Caytori solves this by creating one centralized IT support workflow.

4. Project Objectives
Caytori aims to:
Centralize company IT support requests.
Provide employees with a structured way to report IT issues.
Allow IT personnel to manage and prioritize support requests.
Track tickets from creation to closure.
Maintain a complete history of ticket activity.
Improve communication between employees and IT Staff.
Prevent unauthorized access between companies and users.
Provide companies with IT support analytics.
Reduce lost, forgotten, and duplicated IT requests.
Provide a scalable foundation for future IT service management features.

16. Ticket Business Rules
Rule 1
Every ticket belongs to exactly one company.
Rule 2
Every ticket has one creator.
Rule 3
A ticket may be assigned to one IT Staff member at a time.
Rule 4
Employees can only view their own tickets.
Rule 5
IT Staff can only work on tickets assigned to them.
Rule 6
IT Admin can view and assign company tickets.
Rule 7
IT Staff can mark tickets as resolved.
Rule 8
Employees confirm whether a resolved ticket is actually fixed.
Rule 9
Closed tickets retain their history.
Rule 10
A user's company determines which company data they can access.

The core promise of Caytori
Caytori gives companies one centralized place to report, assign, resolve, track, and analyze IT issues—while keeping employees, departments, and IT support organized within the company's existing structure.
This is the scope I would treat as the baseline specification for the project. It is realistic for actual companies, technically substantial for an IT project, and—most importantly—has clear boundaries so the system doesn't spiral into unnecessary complexity.
